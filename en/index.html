03 · Problem

The training is done,
so why hasn't the work changed?

Every team has its own ways of working, data, and processes, so training alone tends to stop at individual use. AI transformation only starts when AI is fitted to the organization's processes and actually runs in them.

01

No baseline for measuring impact

You can't tell how much work actually got done.

02

Similar tasks still take just as long

The work follows company policy, but processing still takes a long time.

03

No consistent quality checks

With no execution records to compare, quality is hard to judge.

04

Hard to connect to your existing ERP

AI can't reach the system directly, so someone has to click through and upload the results again.

Our review of two years of public records from major listed companies in Korea found the same gap: impact measurement was the one area left empty. A summary of Baryon Labs research BL-IA-2026-03 is available on request.

08 · FAQ

Common questions
before you start

01What exactly does “AI adoption impact measurement” mean?+
Instead of recording only that AI was used, we record which tasks were completed, whether the output was verified, and how time and cost changed, all against the same baseline.
02How is pricing set?+
It depends on the number of seats, the scope of work you connect, and how you need it operated. After a consultation we quote based on your actual scope.
03How is our company data handled?+
We first agree on the data scope and access policy for each task. Where needed, we also design de-identification tools and how your company knowledge is connected.
04What happens to our records and outputs when the contract ends?+
We agree on what is exported, retained, and deleted according to the contract's termination terms, and we explain the difference between your outputs and our service operating data.
05Can this work alongside the AI tools we already use?+
Yes. We look at the web, API, CLI, and desktop touchpoints you use today, then decide which parts to replace and which to connect.
06Do we need to hire dedicated AI staff?+
Not necessarily. Your existing staff can start within the onboarding process, and only the more demanding integrations need to involve your IT team.
