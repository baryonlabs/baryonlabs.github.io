05FAQ

Frequently asked.

What does "Baryon" mean, and why?

Baryons are the heavy particles — protons, neutrons — that everyday matter is made of. We named the lab after the unsexy thing everything else depends on.

How is the AI Work Pipeline priced?

Two stages: a per-seat short onboarding that starts the same day, and an annual subscription with the AX impact report included. Both are quoted by organization size — talk to us.

Can I use your stack with my company's existing tooling?

Yes. miri reads any git repository, miridev-cli ships JSON-RPC and exec modes for CI, and baryon-core is MIT-licensed and provider-agnostic.

Are you hiring?

Quietly, always. If you've shipped an agent in production, send us a link and a sentence — email's at the bottom.

Why both an English and Korean cohort?

The lessons travel but the network is local. Korea has world-class engineers and very few peer cohorts for AI-native building.
