01Service

We run your DGX Spark
so it actually gets used.

Baryon Labs runs a full service around on-premise AI hardware — from a single DGX Spark to a full rack. Five parts, one accountable partner: procurement, managed operations, training, technical support, and consulting.

Procure

Buying is the start, not the end.

We help you choose and buy the right hardware for the job, so the machine that arrives is the one your work actually needs.

  • Requirements review and hardware recommendation
  • Spec and configuration checks, vendor quote coordination
  • Delivery and installation scheduling
Manage

Deployment and day-to-day operations.

We set up the full local stack on your hardware, then keep it running as a managed service.

  • Models, serving, team chat, and developer tooling set up
  • Monitoring, updates, and model upgrades
  • Incident response and regular operations reports
Train

Training for the teams who use it.

Onboarding and hands-on training so local AI becomes part of daily work.

  • Employee onboarding sessions
  • Role-specific workflow training on your actual business
  • Access to our regular online training program
Support

Technical support that stays.

On-site and remote support after the deployment, for as long as the machines are working.

  • On-site and remote support channels
  • Questions answered, problems fixed
  • Environment kept in step as models and tools move fast
Consult

Custom skills and pipelines, built for you.

A separate engagement for teams that need more than operations.

  • Bespoke agent skills designed and built
  • Data pipelines on your local AI stack
  • Workflow automation for your actual processes
